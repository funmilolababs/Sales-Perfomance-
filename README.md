# Sales-Perfomance-
The Sales Performance Dashboard is an Excel-based data analysis project designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance, profitability, customers, products, sales representatives, and monthly customer trends.
The dashboard uses KPIs, charts, and interactive slicers to transform raw sales data into useful business insights that can support management in making data-driven decisions.
**Key Metrics**
Revenue: ₦2.33 Billion
COGS: ₦1.86 Billion
Profit: ₦465.67 Million
Customers: 2,098
**Problem Statement**
How much revenue and profit the business generates.
Which products contribute most to profit.
Which sales representatives generate the highest revenue.
How Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) affects profitability.
How customer numbers change over time.
How sales performance differs across regions, channels, customer types, and categories.
**Solution**
An interactive Sales Performance Dashboard was developed to consolidate key sales metrics and provide visual insights for faster and more informed decision-making.
**Key Skills Demonstrated**
Excel, Data cleaning and preparation, Excel formulas and calculations, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI creation, Data visualization, Dashboard development, Conditional formatting, Bar charts, Column charts, Doughnut chart, Line chart, KPI cards, Interactive slicers, Dashboard layout and design.

1. Revenue vs Profit: The business generated approximately ₦2.33B in revenue and ₦465.67M in profit, while COGS was approximately ₦1.86B.
This shows that a significant portion of revenue is consumed by the cost of goods.
2. Product Performance: Laptop A13 is the highest-performing product by profit at approximately ₦105.34M, followed by Sofa Classic, Desktop PC D21, Smartphone Z10.
3. Sales Representative Performance: Peter generated the highest revenue among the sales representatives at approximately ₦434.81M.
Grace recorded the lowest among the representatives displayed, at approximately ₦367.84M, indicating an opportunity to investigate performance differences.
4. Customer Trend: Customer numbers increased from 520 in January to 536 in April, before dropping sharply to 18 in May.
The May figure should be investigated because it may indicate (Incomplete May data, A reporting issue, A genuine decline in customers).
**Recommendations**
1. Investigate the May Customer Drop: The sharp decrease from 536 customers in April to 18 in May should be investigated immediately to determine whether it is a genuine business decline or a data/reporting issue.
2. Focus on High-Profit Products: Prioritize high-profit products such as Laptop A13, Sofa Classic, and Desktop PC D21 through:
Better inventory planning
Targeted promotions
Marketing campaigns
Appropriate pricing strategies
3. Improve Sales Representative Performance: Analyze the practices of top-performing representatives such as Peter and identify strategies that can be shared with lower-performing representatives.
4. Control COGS: COGS represents a large portion of revenue. Management should review:
Supplier costs
Purchasing strategies
Product margins
Operational costs
Reducing unnecessary costs could significantly improve profit margins.
5. Monitor Regional Performance
Use the Region slicer to compare performance across East, North, South, and West. Resources and marketing efforts can then be directed toward regions with stronger growth opportunities.
6. Analyze Sales Channels: Compare Online vs Store sales to determine which channel generates stronger revenue and profit. This can help guide investment in digital sales and physical stores.
7. Use the Dashboard for Continuous Monitoring: The dashboard should be updated regularly so management can monitor changes in revenue, profit, customers, products, regions, and sales representatives and respond quickly to emerging problems.
**Conclusion**
This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard. The dashboard provides stakeholders with a quick view of business performance while allowing deeper analysis through slicers for Region, Channel, Customer Type, and Category.
